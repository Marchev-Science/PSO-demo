THE IDEA

A search with a shared memory.

Each particle remembers its own best position and learns from the best position found by the swarm. Inertia keeps it moving; two random pulls guide its next step.

v ← w·v + c₁·r₁·(pbest − x)
　　　　 + c₂·r₂·(gbest − x)
x ← x + v

Teal particles explore. The gold cross marks the best position discovered. Pink diamonds use numerical gradients with backtracking line search, starting from the same initial positions.

Try Rastrigin or the terraced surface: local basins and flat regions frustrate gradient search. PSO can jump between them, but can also converge prematurely. Compare several seeds. Iterations are not equal computational budgets; evaluation counts include gradient probes and line searches.
